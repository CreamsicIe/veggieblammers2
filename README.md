# Veggie Blammers 1942

A 3D first-person shooter set in a 1942 victory garden. Every fighter is a vegetable in a wartime helmet, and every weapon squirts juice.

**Play:** https://creamsicie.github.io/veggieblammers2/ in any current browser, on a computer, iPhone, iPad or Android phone. No install and no account.

## Modes

- **Wave Splash** (solo): hold off growing waves of veggies. Every fifth wave brings a giant.
- **Juice Bomb** (bots or online): 6 against 6. One team plants a juice box bomb at site A or B, the other has 30 seconds to defuse it.
- **Splat Fest** (bots or online): 8 fighters, everyone for themselves, first to 15 splats wins.

The garden has two lookouts, one near each team's start. Walk up the hay-bale stairs to a wooden deck 6 high that looks out over most of the garden.

## Playing online

Click **Play with friends → Make lobby**, then send your friends the invite link or the 4-digit code. Up to 8 people can join one lobby.

With **Bots: on** (the host's switch in the lobby), bots fill the empty places, so every Juice Bomb match is 6 against 6 and every Splat Fest has 8 fighters. Friends who join later take a bot's place (in Juice Bomb, from the next round). With bots off, only people play.

The host's browser runs the match, so the host keeps the game open. Players connect to each other directly when they can, and through free relay servers when a network blocks that (phone data, most school or work Wi-Fi). If a connection drops for a moment, the game reconnects by itself.

### If a friend can't join

- **Reload the page**, everyone. Lobbies only work between players on the same version.
- **Opera:** Opera's built-in VPN blocks direct game connections. Turn the VPN off (the VPN badge at the left of the address bar), or open Settings, search for **WebRTC**, and choose **Use any suitable network interface**. Other VPNs and some school or work networks can block the game too.
- **Host on a phone:** the host has to keep the game on screen. Switching apps pauses it; friends wait up to two minutes for the host to come back.

## On iPhone and iPad

The game runs in Safari with touch controls: drag on the left side of the screen to move, drag on the right side to aim, and use the buttons to squeeze, jump, duck and swap. Holding the phone sideways gives the biggest view.

- **Full screen:** in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The game then opens from its own icon without Safari's toolbars.
- **No sound?** iPhone keeps web pages quiet in Silent mode, so turn Silent mode off.
- **Inviting friends:** in a lobby, **Share link** opens the share sheet so you can send the invite by Messages or any chat app.
- **Smooth play:** on slower phones the game lowers its resolution, then turns shadows off, by itself. If the phone resets the 3D graphics (it can happen after the game sits in the background), the game says so and offers a reload.

## Movement

Counter-Strike 1.6 style: ground friction and acceleration, air strafing, bunny hopping (hold Space, then strafe with A or D while turning the same way), crouch-jumping onto higher ledges, footsteps other players can hear, and rocket jumping with the Juice Cannon.

## Controls

WASD move · mouse aim · click squeeze · R new box · 1–9 or scroll to swap · F ice pop bonk · E plant or defuse · Space jump (hold to bunny hop) · Shift walk quietly · C crouch · 6 then jump and fire at your feet to rocket jump · right-click zoom (Long Straw) · Tab scoreboard · Esc pause · N music · M sound

The whole game is the single file `index.html`.
